THE PROJECT IN PLAIN ENGLISH

What Are We Building for Fluff Cakes?

Fluff Cakes is a small local cake business that currently shares cake flavours, celebration prices, and booking rules informally via WhatsApp statuses and word-of-mouth. Customers keep asking the same questions ("How much is a 1 kg birthday cake?", "How many days before do I order?").

Within 14 calendar days, you will individually plan, execute, test, deploy, and formally hand over a crisp, 4-page static informational website hosted at zero cost on GitHub Pages:

  • Page 1 — Home (`index.html`): Hero summary, bakery quick facts, signature sponge highlights, festive announcement.
  • Page 2 — About Us (`about.html`): Fluff Cakes story, freshness standards, public governance roles.
  • Page 3 — Cake Menu & Packages (`services.html`): 1 kg / 2 kg / Cupcake boxes / Two-tier packages + flavour & lead-time table.
  • Page 4 — Order Info & FAQ (`contact.html`): Non-sensitive inquiry email (`mailto:`), pickup hours, and ordering FAQs.
